Sizing decisions should start from what UK capacity is essential – where offshore provision is not an option, it said – and from tasks datacentres perform across the economy, rather than from sector-by-sector demand.</p> <p>“Water is not a major constraint on datacentre siting in the UK,” it said. The notes state that datacentres can be cooled without water using closed-loop systems and ventilation, given the UK’s climate, and that Amazon Web Services’ facilities reportedly run waterless for around 97% of the year.
