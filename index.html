it had developed the new guidance to help defensive teams plan and implement strategies for dealing with adversaries using legitimate stolen credentials, native tools, and other <a intelligence (CTI),” Cisa said in a statement.</p> <p>“As organisations adopt&nbsp;<a target="_blank" href="https://www.cisa.gov/topics/cybersecurity-best-practices/zero-trust"
